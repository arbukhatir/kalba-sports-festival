Frequently asked questions

Everything about registration, passes, points and events.

Registration

How do I register for a championship?

Pick your championship, press Register Now — you instantly get a reg number and QR and can track your status.

What documents are required?

ID for athletes and a medical certificate when required; safety certificate and insurance for kids-zone suppliers.

Can schools take part?

Yes — the Kalba Schools Challenge runs five days of knowledge, sports and creative challenges, with daily leaderboards and a cup for the top school.

Passes & entry

What is the visitor pass?

A free digital QR pass scanned at entry that also collects your points, with a family option up to 12 people.

When and where is the festival?

From 6 to 13 November 2026 on the Kalba Corniche, Emirate of Sharjah, with events extending to other sites across the city.

Points & prizes

How do points work?

An organizer scans your QR after each activity; points appear instantly on the leaderboard and screens.

Where are results and prizes?

The Results page shows live scores, standings and champions. Cups and medals for the top three.

Workshops & events

How do I book a workshop seat?

From the Community page — seats are limited and pre-registration guarantees yours.
