Community events

Take part rather than watch — stations, contests and open sessions, no booking needed.

Daily challenges & calisthenics

Short stations and quick contests — football, throwing games and calisthenics shows, with daily winners announced.

  • Daily 17:00–21:00
  • Community activity yard
  • Visitors, youth and children

Folk games

Traditional game stations that bring the generations together — carrom, tug of war and other heritage games.

  • Daily 16:30–21:30
  • Side games area
  • Families and beginners

Meet the professionals

Open sessions with professional athletes — try the sport up close and ask directly.

  • Thursday & Friday 18:00
  • Main stage
  • Youth and emerging athletes

Honours & closing ceremony

Crowning the champions of all thirteen championships and honouring participants and volunteers.

  • 13 November · 20:00
  • Main stage
  • Everyone
